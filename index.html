About Magnesium Gal

Magnesium Gal is written and reviewed by an editorial team. Articles are researched from published studies and official health guidance, and each one lists its sources at the bottom.

How we write

  • We cite the studies and guidance behind every claim.
  • We say when evidence is limited or more research is needed.
  • We do not give medical advice or tell you what to take.

Nothing here is medical advice. Speak to a doctor or pharmacist about symptoms that worry you, and before starting any supplement, especially if you take medicines or have a health condition.
